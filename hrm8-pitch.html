PERSONAS · 02
HRM8 ATS

Hiring Reimagined.

Two distinct roles. One unified platform. Applicants are not standalone users — they come through the supplier and sit under that supplier's pool.

HR
HR Admin / Hiring Team

The Decision Maker

Posts jobs, configures pipeline stages, evaluates candidates, schedules interviews, and makes final hiring decisions.

→ Kanban pipeline view
→ AI Copilot for all actions
→ Interview scheduling & scorecards
→ Offer & hire management
→ Post jobs as FTE or Contract
→ Dashboard to manage FTE & contract positions
VN
Vendor / Staffing Agency

The Supplier

Maintains a private pool of applicants, submits profiles against client mandates, tracks client decisions, and handles SOWs & invoices. Every applicant lives under the supplier that sourced them — no standalone candidate login.

→ Vendor portal & dashboard
→ Private applicant pool (owned by supplier)
→ Candidate profile & CV submission
→ Real-time status tracking on every submission
→ Invoicing & SOW management
AP
Sub-entity · Lives under Supplier

The Applicant

A profile inside the supplier's private talent pool. The supplier owns the record — adds, updates, and submits it against client mandates. The applicant never logs in.

→ Stored in the supplier's CV pool
→ Submitted by supplier against one or many JDs
→ Status tracked on the supplier's dashboard
→ Interview coordination handled by supplier
→ Placement & billing tied back to the supplier

CUSTOM · 07

Vendor Dashboard

A dedicated portal for every staffing partner. Clients push JDs with a candidate quota, vendors submit profiles, track client decisions, and handle SOWs & invoicing — all in one workspace.

hrm8 · Vendor Portal

Walkthrough of the live Vendor Portal — dashboard, mandate quotas, submission funnel, client activity, invoices & SOWs.

WHAT THE VENDOR SEES
1 · KPI strip at the top
Active mandates, candidates submitted vs. monthly quota, selection rate, and pending invoice value — the vendor's health check in one glance.
2 · Submission funnel
Sourced → Internal Screen → Submitted → Client Shortlist → Placed. Conversion rate at each step tells the vendor where they're leaking candidates.
3 · Active mandates with quotas
Every JD the client pushes comes with a candidate quota (e.g. 10 profiles for Senior Backend). Progress bars show how many are submitted, how many remain, and days to deadline.
4 · Client activity feed
Live updates from the client side — shortlists, interview scheduling, CV revision requests, invoice approvals — so vendors never have to chase status.
5 · Invoices & SOWs
Raise invoices, track Pending / Overdue / Approved / Paid states, manage MSAs and SOWs with versioning and e-signature — no email back-and-forth.
END-TO-END VENDOR FLOW
① Mandate received
Client posts JD + candidate quota to the vendor portal.
② Internal screen
Vendor sources candidates and runs their own screening first.
③ Submission
Shortlisted profiles are pushed to the client with CV, rate & note.
④ Client review
Client picks from vendor's submissions, schedules interviews, decides.
⑤ Bill & close
On placement, vendor raises invoice against the signed SOW.

RBAC · CLIENT-SIDE VISIBILITY
Timesheets & invoices are gated by role. Nobody sees more than they need to — configurable per client.
Hiring Mgr
Sees only consultants on their mandate. Approves / rejects / edits weekly hours.
Finance
Sees approved hours, PO burn, and invoices. Cannot approve hours. Raises / tops-up POs.
Procurement
Owns MSA & SOW docs, vendor onboarding, rate cards. No access to individual hours.
Admin
Full visibility + audit trail. Configures who can see what; can delegate approvals.
Vendor
Can only see their own submissions & invoices. Zero visibility into other vendors.
Default-deny — a role has no access until explicitly granted. Overrides & delegations are scoped per mandate and expire automatically.
