Permission Layer

RBAC Approvals.
Built into the Agent.

Every action the AI agent takes can be gated behind an approval flow — routed to the right person based on role, department, or spend threshold. No one gets more access than they should.

Role-Based Routing
Finance requests go to the CFO. HR requests go to the HR head. Configurable per team.
Threshold-Based Approvals
Amounts above a limit auto-escalate. Below it, the agent acts immediately.
One-tap Approve / Reject in Lark
Approvers get a DM in Lark with full context — approve or reject without leaving the chat.
Full Audit Trail
Every approval, rejection, and escalation is logged — who acted, when, and why.
