About the Role
Stryker's AI & Data Science team is building next-generation prediction models for surgical robotics and medical device performance optimization. We are looking for an experienced ML Engineer to join a cross-functional team and own the end-to-end ML pipeline — from data ingestion through model training, evaluation, and deployment.
Key Responsibilities
Design and implement ML pipelines for real-time inference on surgical data streams
Build and maintain feature stores and data preprocessing workflows at scale
Collaborate with domain experts to translate clinical requirements into model objectives
Set up MLOps practices: model versioning, A/B testing, drift monitoring, retraining pipelines
Deploy and monitor models in production using cloud-native infrastructure (AWS SageMaker)
Requirements
4–8 years of hands-on ML engineering experience (not just data science)
Strong Python — NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow or PyTorch
Experience with MLflow, Weights & Biases, or similar MLOps tooling
AWS or GCP certifications preferred; SageMaker experience is a strong plus
Healthcare or regulated industry experience is a significant advantage
